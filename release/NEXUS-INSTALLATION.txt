F13 Offline Bots - Complete Edition (original/base game)
 
MAIN FEATURES
- Play offline as a counselor against AI Jason, or play as Jason against
  counselor bots using the normal Offline Bots mode.
- Expanded setup menus for BOTH offline counselor and offline Jason play:
  choose your character, Jason, map, counselor bot count and skill, weather,
  Jason difficulty, and 30/60/90-minute match length. Bot counts can exceed
  the stock seven-counselor limit, including ten counselors.
- Offline counselor setup also offers separate starting weapon and starting
  inventory-item choices.
- Hosted online-lobby AI Jason support through your existing compatible
  community online setup. Each fresh launch: open Offline Play, wait about
  five seconds, then return to your hosted lobby and press F8 to arm AI Jason.
  Confirm AI JASON: ARMED before starting; no offline match is needed first.
- During offline counselor and hosted online AI matches, press F1 to spawn
  an additional AI Jason or F3 to spawn additional AI counselor bots.
- Picking up Jason's mask grants sweater power, with recharge support.
  Counselor AI bots converge to help attack Jason when the kill setup activates.
- Jason can be finished with an axe or machete during the valid sweater-stun
  kill window. Timing and the required kill conditions still matter; an
  ordinary hit outside that window is not an automatic kill.
- Multiple-AI-Jason death/ending support preserves the primary kill sequence.
- Runtime Wave replacement with the urination animation/effect for both
  counselor genders, plus synchronized AI-bot emotes in offline counselor
  and hosted online AI play. Bot responses are staggered to limit hitches;
  human players are not forced to join the emote.
- Counselor emote-wheel RB support, while preserving Y for sweater power.
- AI Jason pursuit, Morph/abilities, throwing knives, grab kills, door and
  obstacle recovery, and vehicle interception improvements.
- Living-target spectator filtering and AI Jason spectating support.
  Remote Jason-spectator extensions require the mod on that client's game;
  vanilla remote-player Jason spectating is not guaranteed.
- An extra Offline Challenges setup menu offers unlimited throwing knives,
  faster ability recharge, faster Jason movement, low-detection stealth
  practice, no match timeout, skip intro on replay, counselor escape
  prevention (foot/car), and a disable-Jason-boundary option.
  These challenge-only options do not disable escapes in offline/online bots.
- Dedicated base-game backend with no original game EXE renaming/patching,
  no custom PAK and no -NoPak setup. Supports the exact compatible normal
  SummerCamp-Win64-Shipping.exe and existing community Win64 layouts.
- Installer, verifier, backups and clean uninstaller included.

EXPANDED MENU OPTIONS

OFFLINE BOTS - COUNSELOR
- Counselor selection, Jason selection and map selection.
- Counselor Bot Skill: Low / Medium / High.
- Counselors: 1-10 TOTAL counselors, including your human counselor.
  Selecting 10 starts nine AI counselors plus you, not ten AI bots plus you.
- Weather: Off / Rain / Random (stock weather selection; no added snow/daytime).
- Jason Difficulty: Easy / Normal / Hard.
  Attack and knife damage scaling: half / normal / double. Actual damage can
  still vary with counselor perks and other stock modifiers.
  Rage is earned more slowly / normally / faster; Jason does not start in Rage.
- Match Length: 30 / 60 / 90 minutes.
- Starting Weapon: None / Random / Machete / Axe / Shotgun.
- Starting Inventory: None / Random / Pocket Knife / First Aid Spray /
  Firecrackers / Map. This is separate from the starting weapon.

OFFLINE BOTS - JASON
- Jason selection and map selection.
- Counselor Bot Skill: Low / Medium / High.
- Counselors: stock 1-7 selection.
- Weather: Off / Rain / Random.
- Jason Difficulty: Easy / Normal / Hard.
- Match Length: 30 / 60 / 90 minutes / Unlimited.
- Unlimited Throwing Knives: Off / On.
- Faster Ability Recharge: Off / On.
- Faster Jason Movement (2x): Off / On.
- Starting weapon/inventory rows belong to counselor mode, not Jason mode.

OFFLINE CHALLENGES - ADDITIONAL SETUP SCREEN
Each option is Off / On:
- Unlimited Throwing Knives.
- Faster Ability Recharge.
- Faster Jason Movement (2x).
- Stealth Practice (Low Detection).
- No Match Timeout.
- Skip Intro on Replay.
- Prevent Counselor Escapes: foot and vehicle escape prevention.
- Disable Jason Boundary.
Challenge settings are scoped to challenge missions; they do not block
counselors escaping in normal offline bots or hosted online AI matches.

CONTROLS AND GAMEPLAY NOTES
- F8: arm AI Jason in your hosted online lobby after Offline Play initialization.
- F1 / F3: additional AI Jason / additional AI counselors during supported
  offline counselor and hosted online AI matches. Spawns are bounded, not unlimited.
- Customize your counselor's Wave emote slot to use the runtime urination
  replacement. Both male and female counselors are supported.
- F2: request/enable the Wave-slot urination replacement if it is not ready.
  Wait for it to load before using Wave; F2 is not the emote itself.
- RB: counselor emote-wheel bridge; rear view is retained. Y remains available
  for sweater power. Movement cancels the urination emote; damage/crouching
  also interrupt it. Only AI counselor bots mirror your emote, not humans.
- Pick up the mask to gain sweater power. Recharge and counselor convergence
  support let AI helpers join the attack; eligible axe/machete finishers still
  require the correct sweater-stun kill window.
- Spectator cycling filters dead counselors and adds AI Jason for supported
  modded clients; F4 provides a direct local Jason-camera fallback.
  A host-only installation cannot guarantee this extension for vanilla remotes.

OTHER IMPROVEMENTS
- Resurrected-derived AI foundation adapted to this exact base-game build:
  strategic setup/traps, knives, Morph/abilities, pursuit and close combat.
- Door/obstacle/stuck recovery, grab-kill handling and vehicle interception.
- Shared offline/online AI hunt policy, including the 50-meter Morph threshold.
- Multiple-Jason kill/cleanup handling designed to preserve the main death
  cinematic and correct match outcome, rather than interrupt the kill.
- Counselor-owned HUD/input handoff, preferred character preservation,
  setup-menu reentry and match-state reset improvements.
- Cached lookups, bounded retries, startup-task spacing, reduced idle/controller
  work, and staggered bot emote requests to reduce avoidable hitches.
  No fixed FPS or hitch-free guarantee is made.
- No optional Epstein hook auto-loading or bundled external ESP/radar.
- No extra perk/CP setup rows: use the game's existing customization system.

INSTALLATION
Close the game, extract into the folder containing SummerCamp and Engine,
and run INSTALL-F13-OFFLINE-BOTS.bat. Launch PLAY-F13-OFFLINE-BOTS.cmd or
add that command file as a Steam non-Steam shortcut.
Each fresh game launch: sign in, open Offline Play and wait about five seconds
for the mod to load. No offline match is needed. For online AI, then return to
your hosted lobby and press F8; confirm AI JASON: ARMED before starting.
Going directly to the online lobby without this step leaves F8 inactive.
No executable renaming, manual DLL movement/injection or custom PAK required.
Exact supported executable hash and Steam instructions are in README.txt.
This ZIP does not provide the game or any community networking fix.
Run VERIFY-F13-OFFLINE-BOTS.bat for a shareable diagnostic if anything fails.
Close the game and run UNINSTALL-F13-OFFLINE-BOTS.bat to remove owned files
and restore originals without touching saves. Keep install manifests/backups.
