F13 Offline Bots - Complete Edition 1.0.0-rc1
Original/base-game build, not a drop-in DLL for Resurrected or Camp Forest Green.
Public-release candidate: package/install checks and gameplay checks are separate.

PORTED / ADAPTED FROM THE RESURRECTED MOD WORK
- Custom AI Jason driver and counselor-mode foundation, alongside stock counselor
  bot behavior. Frozen driver source is preserved, with base-game adapters.
- Jason strategic setup/traps, throwing knives, abilities/Morph, close combat,
  grab kills, target pursuit and obstacle/door interaction recovery.
- F8 hosted online-lobby AI Jason arming after Offline Play initialization;
  F1 extra AI Jason and F3 extra AI counselor spawning in offline counselor
  and hosted online AI matches.
- Counselor convergence and extended mask/sweater/Jason-death interaction support.
- Local counselor stat handling and installed-character selection integration.
These describe implemented features, not an assertion that every historical
Resurrected cheat, map, character, hook or asset was imported. The Epstein hook
and Resurrected proxy loader are deliberately not included.

BASE-GAME IMPROVEMENTS / CURRENT FEATURES
- Offline counselor and Jason play surfaces, with integrated runtime menus.
- Counselor skill Low/Medium/High; counts above seven (including tested ten).
- Jason Easy/Normal/Hard damage scaling (half/normal/double inputs) and Rage
  accrual scaling; normal starting state. Armor/perks still affect actual damage.
- 30/60/90-minute offline match length overrides; stock rain Off/On/Random.
  Random means stock weather selection, not added snow/daytime weather.
- Separate starting weapon and small-inventory item settings in counselor mode.
- Startup counselor/Jason selection preservation and menu return/reentry repair.
- Scoped knife launch/startup trap ownership handling; vehicle interception,
  progress-aware chase timeout, shared 50-meter Morph/hunt policy and door/hiding
  spot handling. Match/death bookkeeping handles multiple AI Jasons.
- Sweater access/recharge, mask-triggered kill support and axe/machete finish
  handling; delayed additional-Jason cleanup preserves the primary cinematic.
- Counselor HUD/ownership handoff fixes for hosted AI matches; helper bots use
  high stock skill. Remote players' own preferred character handling is preserved.
- Spectator living-target filtering and AI-Jason support. Modded clients are
  required for client-side extensions; vanilla remote Jason cycling is not promised.
- Wave replaced at runtime by the stock urination animation/effect for both
  counselor genders; bounded retained asset loading, movement cancellation and
  staggered bot mirroring. RB wheel bridge keeps rear view; Y keeps sweater action.
- Challenge-only settings for knives, recharge, Jason attributes, stealth,
  untimed play/replay intro handling and escape/boundary behavior. Challenge
  containment is isolated from offline/online counselor escapes.
- Cached metadata, bounded retries, startup-only maintenance, one mirrored bot
  per 25 ms, startup-task dephasing and cheaper controller/idle/UI work.
- Optional Epstein hook autoload removed; manual injection remains optional.

PACKAGING IMPROVEMENTS IN THIS CANDIDATE
- Dedicated delayed launcher, not version.dll or a KnownDLL-dependent proxy.
- Recognizes normal shipping and existing community process names and exact paths.
- Exact executable SHA256/x64/profile checks, game-folder/content checks,
  source/copy hash checks, backup journal, install manifest and reversible upgrade.
- No executable renames, game-content patching, registry/System32 edits, -NoPak,
  manually moved DLLs or user-operated injection steps.
- Shareable diagnostics distinguish files copied, module loaded, backend session
  and menu-hook evidence. Unsupported/missing/quarantined files fail visibly.
- Source-only archive, compact runtime archive, licenses/notices and SHA256s.
- Preserves existing OnlineFix files; networking fixes/game assets are not shipped.

TESTING / LIMITATIONS
Existing user tests confirmed many features on the prior installed base build.
The final packaging build still needs fresh-profile, three-character selection,
five-menu-cycle and remote-computer gameplay checks. See TEST-MATRIX.txt.
Do not present a compiled or file-installed candidate as a fully tested release.
